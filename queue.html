How to join

  1. Follow twitch.tv/waxysplash and come to a Pro Clubs stream.
  2. When the queue is open, type !join in chat. That's it: WaxyBot knows who you are from your Twitch name. Add anything you like after it (your position, your EA name), e.g. !join ST.
  3. When WaxyBot tags you, watch for the Waxys Phantoms invite in EA FC and accept it.
In chat: !join !leave !queue · Positions: GK CB LB RB CDM CM CAM LM RM LW RW ST, or ANY.
